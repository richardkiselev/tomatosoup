Tomato Soup

A classic comfort food | M. Yinseng

Source: https://yingsengeats.com/tomato-soup

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup vegetable broth
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Chili powder
  • Optional, fresh chilies

Instructions:

  1. In a large pot, sauté the onion and garlic until fragrant. If using chilies, add them in after a minute.
  2. Add the diced tomatoes and vegetable broth.
  3. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 20 minutes.
  4. Stir in the oregano, salt, and pepper.
  5. Blend the soup until smooth (optional).

About This Recipe:

Tomato soup is a classic comfort food with a deep tomato flavor and a light combination of vegetables and herbs. My rendition uses vegetable broth, garlic, onion, and oregano, as well as my secret ingredient: chili powder! The blend of spices adds a little bit of kick to an otherwise smooth soup and brings in that smokey flavor I love. Whenever I'm sick, I add lots of chili powder (and sometimes some fresh chilies from my garden), and this soup clears my sinuses up instantly. I'll be licking the bowl clean with snot pouring out of my nose... anyway, bon appetit!
